# Data and code availability — text for the manuscript

**Data availability.** The per-crown leaf-presence time series of the 23,163 automatically delineated
and 969 field-identified crowns, the crown polygons, the species and leaf
habit of the field-identified crowns, their terrain descriptors, the daily climate series, the labels
and DINOv2 features of the 199,681 photo-interpreted points, the fitted leaf-presence model and the leaf-presence probability maps of every acquisition (27 at
Luki, 46 at Mbalmayo, 44 at Yangambi; GeoTIFF rasters and an overview document) are available from
the University of Liège repository at https://dox.uliege.be/s/XdLtrctZD2N9MiJ. The UAV orthomosaics
are available through the Canopy Observatories network (CanObs, https://www.canobs.net) upon request.

**Code availability.** The code of the processing chain (DINOv2 feature extraction, leaf-presence
model, probability maps, per-crown time series) and of all analyses is available at https://github.com/APlumacker/canopy-deciduousness under an MIT
licence. Running the analysis scripts on these data reproduces the figures and tables of the article.
